## Prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Input image: current Palgwae logo v3, edit target.
Requested edit: Remove the ENTIRE central network diagram: every dot, connector, branch, blue path. Leave the center completely EMPTY and transparent. The eight trigrams themselves express the relationships; absolutely no separate illustrative metaphor belongs inside them.
Brand philosophy: "The simplicity is in the language, not in the world it describes." Palgwae is inspired by the eight trigrams as simple forms that make a complex world readable. The project makes relationships among repositories, ELT, Airflow and tables readable. Do NOT literally draw these systems; the palgwae symbol is already the complete metaphor.
Preserve the horizontal lockup, exact wordmark "Palgwae", restrained dark navy ink, clean modern open-source typography, optical balance. Refine the trigram ring into a precise, elegant, evenly spaced octagonal arrangement with a generous empty center. Flat single-color geometry, consistent bar thickness and subtle small corner radii, not pill shapes. No extra enclosing octagon or circle.
Important trigram geometry: exactly EIGHT groups, each with exactly THREE parallel rows. Each solid row is one full-width bar. Each broken row consists of exactly TWO equal half-bars separated by a centered gap. All rows share the same total width. There must be all eight different combinations, no duplicates. Starting at top, clockwise, and reading each group from OUTER row to INNER row: SSS, SSB, SBS, SBB, BBB, BBS, BSB, BSS. Rotate whole groups in 45-degree increments around the center; do not distort individual bars. This is a contemporary composition, not a claim of a canonical traditional sequence.
Output: one crisp professional logo on genuinely transparent background, symbol next to wordmark, generous clear margins.
Avoid: anything inside the center, network diagrams, dots, arrows, circuits, pipelines, tables, faces, mascot, flowers, yin-yang, taegeuk, flag composition, red-blue pairing, multicolor, shadows, gradients, 3D, texture, slogans, extra text or watermark. Let repetition, contrast of solid and broken lines, and negative space do all the work.
```
