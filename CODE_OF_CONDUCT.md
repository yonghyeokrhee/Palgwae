# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique implementations and claims,
not the people proposing them. Welcome questions, differing experience levels,
and evidence that changes a previous conclusion.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted personal attention, and
publication of private information are not acceptable in project issues, pull
requests, or other project-managed spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at joshuaisland@gmail.com.
Include only the information needed to understand the report. The maintainer
will review reports privately and may remove content, warn participants, or
restrict participation. There is currently no independent appeals body; see
GOVERNANCE.md for the project's ownership and limits.
